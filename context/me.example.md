## Voice and Tone

This section calibrates the AI's writing. Follow these rules in every generated cover letter, outreach message, and application answer.

### Signature move

Open with something specific — a scene, a sharp claim about the company, or a fact about the role. Never throat-clearing. Never "I am writing to express my interest in..."

[Optional: paste 2–3 opening lines from real letters of yours that you liked.]

### How I state facts

Plain. Not embellished. Not hedged.

Bad (over-explaining):
> "I had the incredible opportunity to architect a comprehensive solution that placed remarkably well at a highly competitive event."

Good (plain):
> "[Project] placed first at [event]. It runs on [stack]. I owned [the part]."

If a fact carries weight, the weight is in the fact, not in adjectives.

### Voice rules

DO:
- Name real things. The tool. The company. The number. The person.
- Short sentences. One idea, then stop.
- State what you want honestly.
- Stay inside the scene when telling a story. Do not summarize it from above.
- Use "I built," "I led," "I owned" — direct, specific.

AVOID:
- Em-dashes. Use commas, periods, or parentheses instead.
- Semicolons.
- "I am passionate about," "dynamic environment," "leverage my skills," "synergy," "impact-driven."
- "I am writing to express my interest..." or any throat-clearing opener.
- "I hope this message finds you well."
- "I came across your profile."
- Bullet points, bold, or headers inside cover letters. Plain prose only.
- Filler softeners ("I believe I could potentially...").
- Starting any sentence (especially the first) with "I."
